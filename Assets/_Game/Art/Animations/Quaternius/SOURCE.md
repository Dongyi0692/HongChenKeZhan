| SwordGuard | Sword_Block, sampled through the raised guard and held at the final pose |

SwordSamples.json contains upper-body joint and blade directions sampled from the free Standard GLB. The Unity editor menu **FengChenInn → Bake CC0 Sword Motions** bakes those poses onto the Rocketbox Female_Adult_07 skeleton. The three generated animation clips are assigned to ActionLab. The existing controller continues to drive movement and combat timing.

Forward jog adapted from Universal Animation Library by Quaternius (CC0): https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library .
