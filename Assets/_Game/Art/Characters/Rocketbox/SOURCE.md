# Female_Adult_07

Microsoft Rocketbox, adult avatar `Female_Adult_07`. The original FBX and five TGA texture maps are retained alongside the MIT license. Unity uses local URP materials that reference these maps.

- [Official asset directory, revision 0943055](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/Assets/Avatars/Adults/Female_Adult_07)
- [Upstream MIT license](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/blob/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/LICENSE.md)

The ActionLab scene drives movement through its procedural pose system and plays separately baked CC0 clips on the upper body during sword attacks and guard.
